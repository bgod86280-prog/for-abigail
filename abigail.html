Dear Abigail,

I'm really sorry for the things I've done and for the times I've hurt you. I know saying "sorry" doesn't magically fix everything, and I don't expect it to.

I just want you to know that I genuinely care about you. I don't want our relationship to end because of my mistakes.

I'm not going to promise that I'll suddenly become perfect, because I know that's not realistic. But I can promise that I'll try my best to do better. I'll listen more, understand you better, and work on the things I've been doing wrong.

I don't want to force you to stay or make you feel like you have to forgive me immediately. I just want a chance to show you that I can learn from my mistakes.

If there's still a little part of you that wants us to work, I'd really like to try again — properly this time.

Whatever you decide, I just wanted you to know how much you mean to me.

I'm sorry, Abigail ❤️
— Evin
